What is SonsWo?

SonsWo is a simple space created for sons who are dealing with absent parents, difficult family situations, or who just want to talk honestly with other guys who get it. No judgment. No lectures. Just a place to be heard and hear others.

Why sons come here

No mother or father

Growing up (or still living) without one or both parents and needing to talk about what that feels like.

Family issues

Tension, distance, addiction, conflict, or silence at home that you don’t know how to process.

Just want to talk

Sometimes you just need other sons who understand without having to explain everything from the start.
